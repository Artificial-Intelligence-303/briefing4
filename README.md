# Briefing 4: Allegheny AI Perspectives (CMPSC 303)

This repository is your group's fourth briefing. Your group interviews four people on campus, compares
what you heard against how this class answered the same questions in Week 1, and gives a 3-5 minute
presentation.

| What | Where | Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Your interview data | `interviews.csv`, one row per interview | Yes |
| Your written briefing | `briefing.md`, in this folder, with that exact name | Yes |
| Your visual | `figure.png` (or `.jpg`, or `.svg`), in this folder | Yes |

All three are due **by 11:00am on Thursday, November 12**, the start of class, which is when you will
present. Push them to this repository.

## Groups

**You choose your own group, of three or four people.**

## Who you interview

**Your group finds its own four people:**

- **Three students**, none of them in this class and none of them your roommate, from at least two class
  years and at least two divisions (humanities, social science, natural science).
- **One faculty member, staff member, or administrator**, from outside the Department of Computer and
  Information Science. Email to schedule, and keep it to ten minutes.

Ask each person whether another group has already interviewed them. If one has, find someone else.

`interview-protocol.md` has what to say first, the questions, and how to record the answers.

## What is in this repository

| File | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| `interview-protocol.md` | The script and questions for every interview |
| `interviews.csv` | Where you record the answers, with no names |
| `class-survey.md` | How this class answered the same questions in Week 1 |
| `briefing.md` | The template for your written briefing |

## This round stays inside the course

**Your briefing and your data are not published on the course website.** You told the people you
interviewed that nothing leaves the course, so this repository stays private and nothing from it gets
posted anywhere else.

**You do not need to make slides.** You will present from `briefing.md` and your visual on GitHub.

## What this briefing practices

**Outcome 5:** "Evaluate intelligent systems while considering their social, political, and ethical
implications and communicate their outcomes in both written and oral forms." You collect what people on
this campus understand and do with AI, compare it against this class, and brief the class on what you
found.

## How to fill it in

1. Read `interview-protocol.md` and `briefing.md` before your first interview.
2. Add one row to `interviews.csv` after each interview.
3. Fill in `briefing.md`, using `class-survey.md` for the comparison.
4. Put your image in this folder and name it `figure.png`, `figure.jpg`, or `figure.svg`.
5. Commit and push. Check that all three files appear on GitHub.

## What this is graded on

Three points, split three ways.

| Component | Points | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The written brief | 1 | Four interviews recorded in `interviews.csv` that meet the rules above. Every field in `briefing.md` answered specifically. Your visual is present and says what data it shows. |
| The analysis | 1 | Each pattern is supported by your data, compared against the class numbers, and stated with its counts. You say what four interviews cannot tell you. |
| The oral briefing | 1 | Graded individually on clarity. Each team has three to five minutes, and time to answer a question afterwards. |

## How this assignment was built

I designed this round so that the class survey from Week 1 and the campus interviews ask the same
questions and the two can be compared. I used Claude to draft this README, `interview-protocol.md`,
`class-survey.md` and `briefing.md`.

## Using AI on this

Allowed, disclosed, and your responsibility. Full policy: **https://areweagentsyet.com/ai/**

**Every row in `interviews.csv` is a conversation someone in your group had**, and every count in your
brief needs to match that file. Do not put an interview's notes into an AI tool with anything that could
identify the person.
