# Briefing 4: Allegheny AI Perspectives (CMPSC 303)

For this briefing, your group talks with six people on campus about how they understand and use AI. You
then compare what you heard with how this class answered the same questions in Week 1, and you share what
you found in a 3-5 minute presentation.

| What | Where | Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Your interview notes | `interviews.csv`, one row per interview | Yes |
| Your written briefing | `briefing.md`, in this folder, with that exact name | Yes |
| Your visual | `figure.png` (or `.jpg`, or `.svg`), in this folder | Yes |

All three are due **by 11:00am on Thursday, November 12**, the start of class, which is when you will
present. Push them to this repository.

## Your group

Please choose your own group of five or six people.

## Who to interview

Your group finds its own six people, and at least one of them should be a faculty member, a staff member,
or an administrator. The rest can be students who are not in this class, from at least two different class
years. Roommates and friends are welcome.

**At most one of your six can come from the Mathematics and Natural Sciences area of study**, which
includes Computer Science. We want to hear from the parts of campus this class does not usually reach.

Allegheny groups its majors and departments into six areas of study. `interview-protocol.md` lists them,
and you can also find them in the
[Academic Bulletin](https://catalog.allegheny.edu/content.php?catoid=58&navoid=2463). Record each person's
area of study as you go, because your briefing will describe where your interviewees came from.

Before you begin an interview, ask whether another group has already talked with this person. If one has,
thank them and find someone else. When you approach a faculty member, staff member, or administrator, please
email ahead to schedule a time and keep the conversation to about ten minutes.

## What gets published

Your briefing and your visual are published at **areweagentsyet.com/briefings/**, and you will present from
your briefing's page on the website, so you do not need to make slides.

`interviews.csv` stays in this private repository because it holds people's names. Please keep interviewees'
names and any details that could identify them out of `briefing.md`, your visual, and your presentation.

## What this briefing practices

**Outcome 5:** "Evaluate intelligent systems while considering their social, political, and ethical
implications and communicate their outcomes in both written and oral forms." You learn how people on this
campus understand and use AI, compare that with this class, and tell the class what you found.

## How to fill it in

1. Read `interview-protocol.md` and `briefing.md` before your first interview.
2. Add a row to `interviews.csv` after each conversation.
3. Fill in `briefing.md`, using `class-survey.md` for the comparison. Keep every `##` heading and every
   `- Label:` line as it is and write your answers after them, because the course website reads this file
   directly.
4. Put your image in this folder and name it `figure.png`, `figure.jpg`, or `figure.svg`.
5. Commit and push, then check that all three files appear on GitHub.

## What this is graded on

Three points, split three ways.

| Component | Points | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The written brief | 1 | Six interviews are recorded in `interviews.csv` and meet the rules above. Every field in `briefing.md` is answered specifically. Your visual is present and says what data it shows. |
| The analysis | 1 | Each pattern is supported by your data, compared with the class numbers, and stated with its counts. You describe where your interviewees came from and what six interviews cannot tell you. |
| The oral briefing | 1 | Graded individually on clarity. Each group has three to five minutes, and time to answer a question afterwards. |

## How this assignment was built

I designed this round so that the Week 1 class survey and the campus interviews ask the same questions and
can be compared. I used Claude to look up the areas of study in the Academic Bulletin, to total the class
survey results, and to draft this README, `interview-protocol.md`, `class-survey.md` and `briefing.md`.

## Using AI on this

AI tools are allowed, as long as you disclose how you used them and take responsibility for the result. The
full policy is at **https://areweagentsyet.com/ai/**.

Each row in `interviews.csv` should come from a conversation someone in your group really had, and the counts
in your brief should match that file. Please remove people's names before you share any interview notes with
an AI tool.
