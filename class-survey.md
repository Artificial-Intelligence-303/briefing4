first nine questions in `interview-protocol.md` ask the same things, so you can compare your interviews with
these numbers. Because this class has 23 people and your group has six interviews, comparing percentages or
"how many of six" will be fairer than comparing raw counts.

## Q9. What is one thing AI should never do?

This question was optional, and 19 people answered it. Nine of them said AI should not replace people, creative
work, or human thinking. Three said it should not hold power or make decisions over people's lives.
